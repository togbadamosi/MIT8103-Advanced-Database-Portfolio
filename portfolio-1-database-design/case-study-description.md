# Portfolio 1: Database Design and Modelling
## Case Study: ApexPetro Energy Limited
ApexPetro Energy Limited is a fictional oil and gas company created solely for this academic portfolio. The proposed Operations & Maintenance Management System is a database-driven system for managing information relating to the company's operational assets, equipment, maintenance activities, inspections, equipment failures and spare-parts utilisation.

The database will support maintenance personnel, operations personnel, engineers, inventory personnel, supervisors and system administrators in recording, retrieving and managing operational and maintenance information.

Major data maintained by the system will include assets, equipment, employees, work orders, maintenance activities, inspections, equipment failures, spare parts, inventory transactions, vendors and maintenance costs.

Key database operations will include registering equipment, creating and updating work orders, recording maintenance and inspection activities, tracking equipment failures, managing spare-parts usage, retrieving equipment maintenance history and analysing maintenance activities and costs.

All data used for this case study will be synthetic and created solely for academic purposes.
