# Portfolio 1: Database Design and Modelling
## Case Study: ApexPetro Energy Limited
ApexPetro Energy Limited is a made up oil and gas company used for the purpose of an academic portfolio. The proposed system called Operations & Maintenance Management System is a system that manages information relating to the company's operational assets, equipment, maintenance activities, inspections, breakdown of equipment and the use of spares using a database.

The database will enable maintenance people, operations people, engineers, inventory people, supervisors and system administrators to record, retrieve and manage information about operations and maintenance.

Major data maintained by the system will include assets, equipment, employees, work orders, maintenance activities, inspections, equipment failures, spare parts, inventory transactions, vendors and maintenance costs.

Some of the key database functions will be equipment registration, work order creation and update, maintenance and inspection log, monitoring of equipment failures, management of spare parts usage, and retrieval of equipment maintenance history and analysis of maintenance activities and costs.

All data used for this case study will be synthetic and created solely for academic purposes.
